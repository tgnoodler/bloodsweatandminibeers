The Headline

Shut Out Of The Trade Market, Craig Turns To The Wire To Stay Out Of The Bib

The busiest owner in the league is 3-11, and the trade market he needs does not exist.

Craig lost to Tanner by 45 on Sunday. He is 3-11 after seven weeks, and nobody in the league has had more points scored against him. He is two games behind Hayden and PJ, the next two up. Nobody is mathematically last yet. The sim gives him about a one in three chance of carrying the bags, the worst number anyone has.

His answer has been to shop. He has been the most active owner on waivers for a month. This week he outbid Tanner for Xavier Worthy, signed Darnell Washington on Wednesday, cut him Sunday for Hunter Henry, lost the Bengals defense to PJ by a dollar and bid on Omar Cooper, who went to Sko for more. Earlier this year he bought Chris Godwin, two defenses and Darius Cooper, who scored nothing and was released.

The roster is not the problem. He has Ja'Marr Chase, George Kittle and Chuba Hubbard, and any contender would start all of them. Nobody has called. This league has not completed a trade all season and the deadline is Week 11. Caleb Williams outscored Jordan Love from his bench on Sunday. He plays PJ this week and Hayden in Week 12, and those two games decide the bib. Last year it was Sko, who put his phone number on the golf balls.

Ian Is A Better Team When He Owes A Smirnoff
He replaced an empty slot with the Panthers, lost three points on the deal, and won by 61 anyway. He has four seasons of this.

Head to head, regular season, 2023 to now. On the dotted line an Ice makes no difference to a man. Ian is 11-3 when iced and about .550 the rest of the time.

Two weeks ago Ian left a lineup slot empty. An empty slot scores zero, which is an Ice, and he lost to Brad. He signed the Panthers defense so it would not happen again. On Sunday the Panthers scored minus three. The empty slot would have beaten them. He owes the same Smirnoff either way, and this time he spent a roster spot on it.

He also beat Hayden by 61. Ian with a Smirnoff Ice coming is a better fantasy team than Ian without one, and the record says so. Since 2023 he has been iced in 14 different weeks, third most in the league. He is 11-3 in those weeks, 79 percent. Across every week he has played, three and a half seasons of them, he wins about 54 percent. The rest of the league together wins 40 percent of its iced weeks. The only Ice that has ever cost him a game was the empty slot.

He owes the bottle by noon Sunday. He plays Pelan at noon Sunday and is favored. Pelan owes one too.

Two Bottles In Seven Minutes: Pelan Beats One Clock And Starts Another
One bottle due at 3:25, a second coming together on the field, both drunk with minutes left.

Pelan started Sunday owing a bottle. DJ Moore had given him nothing the week before, and the deadline was 3:25 in the afternoon. That left plenty of time. He also had the 49ers defense in his lineup, kicking off in Atlanta at noon. They sat on zero and stayed there.

The chat watched two things at once. The 49ers did not move. The clock did. At 3:16 the first video arrived, nine minutes clear of the deadline and the closest anyone has come to a penalty this season. At 3:23 the second one arrived. It was for the 49ers, whose game was still going and who were still at zero. He drank for an Ice that had not finished happening, seven minutes after the first video and still inside the first video's deadline. The second one was better than the first.

Then he beat Brad 141 to 76, with the 49ers at zero in his lineup and Dak Prescott and Blake Corum on the bench. The 49ers bottle is due by noon this Sunday. He plays Ian at noon this Sunday. Ian also owes a bottle by noon Sunday.
