The Headline

20

Twenty starters have left a game early this season, the most through four weeks since the league began, and Sunday was the worst day of it. Rashee Rice lasted six snaps. Jalen Nailor lasted nineteen. Ladd McConkey lasted twenty-four. None of them scored, and Hayden and Ethan are drinking three Ices between them for lineups that were fine at kickoff.

Price, Goedert and Barkley have all left early and finished within two points of a bottle. As of this morning eleven rostered players are listed Out, three are on IR and ten more are Questionable.

Former Jacket Winners Finally Show Signs Of Life
Ty, Pelan and Will were a combined 3-15 through three weeks; on Sunday they posted three of the four best scores in the league

Ty, Pelan and Will came into Sunday a combined 3-15. Ty put up the second-best week of his career, Will had his best week since Week 14 of last year, and Pelan set his own season high, launching the former champions into three of the top four spots of the week. Together they had been averaging 107 a week. On Sunday they averaged 172.

It came from big games, not deep ones. Five of the ten best individual scores in the league on Sunday belonged to these three teams: McMillan's 45.2 for Will was the biggest week by any player this season, Ty had four starters clear 26, Kyren and Javonte Williams at the top of the list, and Pelan got 20 or more out of four different guys, Nacua first among them.

Unfortunately for Pelan it wasn't enough, as he faced Ty, the leading scorer of the week. Craig, who left the draft with a B and the sixth-best roster on paper, now rounds out the bottom, his only win of the year coming from the median in Week 3.

The Case For The Kicker
He was saved by a kicker. Now he wants yours gone.

There is a man in this league who wants to abolish the kicker. He will tell you kickers are random. He will tell you nobody has ever won anything with one. He will tell you this with a straight face, because he has forgotten the name Jake Bates, and he would prefer that you never learn it.

In Week 4 of last year, Ty beat PJ by five points. Play that game under Ty's own rule, no kickers for anybody, and PJ wins by three. Ty finishes the year alone in last place and spends this past August in a white jumpsuit carrying Will's clubs while golf balls with his phone number on them roll down the fairway. Sko did the time instead, and Ty has never once said thank you.

So when Ty tells you kickers don't matter, understand what he is asking. He is asking you to forget the one afternoon his own rule would have put him in the jumpsuit, and he is asking because he was the one it saved.

Ty saysThe record says
“Kickers are random.”A kicker has decided 15 head-to-head games since 2023, one every 18.
“They don't affect the standings.”The playoff field was different in 2024, 2025 and, as it stands, 2026.
“It's not a real position.”12 Ices have been served by kickers. Sko drank one at -2.0 last year.
“There's no edge in it.”The gap between the best and worst kicker in an average week is 15.4 points.
“I've never needed one.”He needed exactly one. Jake Bates, Week 4, 2025. See above.
The Mini Beer Minute remembers Jake Bates. Vote no.
