The Headline

Smirnoff Shares Tumble On Reports Of A Sober Sunday In Kansas City

Analysts trace the collapse to a fantasy league in Kansas City that Smirnoff lists in its annual report as a key customer.
30
Straight weeks with an Ice, until this one.

Markets for flavored malt beverages opened in hysteria on Monday after reports that the Blood, Sweat & Mini Beers league had managed to play fantasy football for once. Every lineup in the league went through Thursday, Sunday and Monday night without a starter at zero, which had not happened since the Sunday before Halloween in 2024.

It was not for lack of trying. Craig had an Ice on the board until Carnell Tate finally caught one with three minutes and nine seconds left in the fourth, and PJ carried Harold Fannin at zero into the final minute before Fannin got on the sheet with fifty-six seconds to play. Five owners started a defense that barely scored, and twelve starters in all finished with two points or less, the most near misses in any week since league inception.

Some speculators believe market manipulation is at play. The first time this league went a week without an Ice, there were ten Ices in the following two weeks. Smirnoff declined to comment.

John T Is Good And Cursed; Sko Is Bad And Blessed
One of them deserves better, and the other keeps catching his opponent on their worst week.

John T has scored more per week than anyone who has ever played in this league and has never won it, because the man across from him keeps having his best day. Ty did it to him in the 2023 final, Pelan in the 2024 final, Will in last year's semifinal, and this year the whole league is taking turns. He has had 764 points scored against him this season, the most of any owner.

Sko has scored fewer points than anyone in the league and is .500, because four of the five men he has played had their worst week of the year against him, Will's being the lowest score anyone has posted this season. On Monday night he trailed PJ by fifteen with nothing left to play, and Davante Adams got him there. He said in August, in the jumpsuit, that it was the last time, and nobody has been able to prove him wrong.

Twelve Owners Would Rather Lose The League Than Lose A Trade

Nobody in this league trades. There were no trades in 2023, two in 2024 and two in 2025, PJ was in three of them, and that makes him the most active dealer in the league by a margin of two. This year there have been a hundred and three roster moves and not one of them involved a second owner. The excuse is always the same, which is that nobody wants to be the guy who lost the trade in the chat, and the reality is that of the four trades this league has ever made, only two were meaningfully bad for anybody.
