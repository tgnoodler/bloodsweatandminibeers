The Headline

League Leader Calls Opponent “Too Scared To Play Me,” Then Loses To His Nine-Man Lineup

One week after scoring 213, Tanner scored 90, lost by 23 and drank his first Ice of the season.
Sunday, Nov. 15

Tanner scores 213.62, the most by anyone this season. He is in first place. He is also the only owner in the league who has not had to drink an Ice this year. The other eleven have.

Wednesday

The Minute’s forecast has Tanner beating Pelan 131.5 to 97.0 and lists him as a -450 favorite.

Thursday

The Colts lose to the Texans, 26–3. Tanner has two Colts in his lineup, a running back and the kicker. They finish with 2.8 points between them.

By Saturday

Pelan has five players on a bye and one lineup spot he cannot fill without cutting somebody. He decides not to. He leaves the spot empty and accepts the Ice that comes with it, because he expects to lose either way.

Saturday

Tanner, who has 2.8 points from two players, tells the group chat that Pelan is “too scared to play me this week.”

Sunday, noon

Dallas wins 51–30, and both men have a Cowboy in the game. Pelan’s is the quarterback, who scores 35.9. Tanner’s is a receiver, who scores 2.1. Another of Tanner’s receivers, DK Metcalf, scores 1.2.

Sunday night

Tanner trails by 20 with two players left: Christian McCaffrey of the 49ers and the Vikings defense, the highest-scoring defense in fantasy football this season. They are playing each other.

Late Sunday night

The 49ers win 35–10. McCaffrey scores 14.2 for Tanner. The Vikings defense, having allowed 35 points, scores zero. Pelan’s kicker, who was in the same game, scores five.

Moments later

The zero from the Vikings defense means Tanner owes an Ice, his first of the season. He was the last owner in the league without one. The only other Ice in his game belongs to Pelan, who took his on purpose.

Monday

The game ends 114.30 to 90.86. Tanner’s score is the lowest in the league this week. Pelan wins by 23 with nine players.

Tuesday

Tanner is still in first place and would like that noted.

Bubble Watch: Coast Guard Monitoring Lifeboat Built For Three, Carrying Six
Three owners have already been rescued. Three never reached the boat. That leaves six men and three seats.

The Coast Guard confirmed Tuesday that it is monitoring a lifeboat built for three and carrying six, with three weeks left in the regular season. Six teams make the playoffs. Tanner, Ian and Brad are safely in, which leaves three spots.

John T and Hayden, both 12-10, have seats. Authorities noted that Hayden has scored fewer points than any of the other five and could not say how he got one.

Ty, 11-11, has the third seat. Pelan is also 11-11 and is holding on to the side. Ties go to points, and he trails Ty by eight.

PJ, 10-12, is in the water, one game back. Ethan, 9-13, is two games back and holding a rope.

Conditions are expected to worsen this week. PJ and Pelan play each other. So do Ty and Ethan. John T draws Brad, who has scored 144 or more three weeks in a row. Hayden draws Craig, who is in last place, a development the Coast Guard called "consistent with everything else that has happened to Hayden."

Will, Sko and Craig were not on the lifeboat. They are out of the playoff race and have a different problem, which is that last place wears the bib.

Sign Crew Told To Be Ready Friday As Pelan Bets His Team Name On Nebraska
One game is for a playoff spot. The other is Iowa–Nebraska, and it is for the name on a fantasy team.

PJ and Pelan meet twice this weekend. The commissioner and the editor of this newsletter, who sit in second and first place and are both Iowa fans, do not care who wins the fantasy game.

In fantasy, PJ is 10-12 and Pelan is 11-11, and both are chasing the last playoff spot. The loser is in real trouble.

The other game is Iowa–Nebraska on Friday, PJ’s team against Pelan’s. They agreed to a bet in the group chat this week in the time it takes to type “Fantasy team name bet Friday” and “Deal.” The winner gets to rename the loser’s fantasy team.

This matters more to one of them. Pelan’s team is called QB Kneel Merchant. He named it in Week 1, after he beat PJ by less than a point on a kneel-down, and PJ has had to look at it for eleven weeks. If Iowa wins, PJ picks the new name. He said he is “clearing my schedule this week to brainstorm team names.”

They have done this before. PJ won, and Pelan’s team spent the rest of the season as Bret Bielema’s Calf Tattoo.

The terms are already in dispute. PJ says the loser cannot change the name back until after the next draft. Pelan’s reply was “No,” and then that PJ “didn’t abide by that last year.”

The bet came at the end of a long group chat argument about how many national championships Iowa has won. Pelan sent a screenshot of a Google search for the 1960 national champion and wrote “Seriously congrats.” Sko added “1921 and 1922!” and then “1856 National Champions.” Pelan said he could not “keep up with your moral victories without trophies.” PJ asked how anyone could have a national title in November. Nobody answered him.

Tanner tried to change the subject to his own game. That did not go well either.

The sign crew has been told not to start until Friday.
