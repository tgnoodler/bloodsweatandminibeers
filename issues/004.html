The Headline

Defending Champion Embarrasses Himself Again In A Record-Breaking Loss

68.06
The lowest score in league history. Will, Week 3.

Last week this space said the defending champion was the man most likely to carry the bag next summer. He read it, and on Sunday he went out and made it official. Will scored fewer points than any team in the history of this league. Not this season. Ever. He lost to Sko, who is not good, by the widest margin of the week, and he is the only owner still without a win of any kind, head to head or against the cut.

It is not for lack of trying. He has spent more waiver money than anyone in the league, most of it on defenses, and the one he started this week finished the day at a number a kicker would apologize for. The jacket is in his closet. Smart money says it doesn't stay there long.

Craig Is Owed One

Newborn Marshall is not the only reason Craig is sleepless. Craig is 1-5, and it is not a 1-5 team. Against the whole field he is 13-20, which with a normal schedule is a 3-3 team. Every week he has drawn the one opponent who could beat him.

This week he put up his best score of the season and still lost to Brad, and the reason was one lineup call: he started Travis Etienne over Jakobi Meyers, and Meyers outscored Etienne by more than the final margin.

Next he plays an undefeated Ian, whom Craig holds a 2-1 record over. Our line says Ian. Somebody has to hand Ian his first loss, and nobody is owed it more than Craig.
