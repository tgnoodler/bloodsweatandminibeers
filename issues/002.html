The Headline

PJ Lost To A Guy Taking A Knee

0.30
Margin of defeat. PJ to Scott. The closest game in league history.

PJ paid forty-two dollars for De'Von Achane and got a fraction of it back. He started Harold Fannin over Isaiah Likely and gave away a game's worth of points at one position. He left more on his bench than some teams scored with their starters. And after all that he was still winning, right up until Jaxson Dart, his one dollar quarterback who had just played the game of his life, knelt down to run out the clock and lost him the week.

Scott, who beat him, renamed his team QB Kneel Merchant that night.
