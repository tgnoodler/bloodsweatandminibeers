Two Contenders, Nine Coin Flips And Sko: The League At Week Six
Tanner and Brad are 88 points clear of third, the next nine are 76 points apart top to bottom, and twelfth is 54 behind eleventh.
By Ty

After six weeks, the league standings are beginning to shape up. Based on points scored, Tanner and Brad are showing to be the league blue bloods of the year. 960 and 887 respective points scored, with no other team breaking 800 after six weeks. Is this a two man show, or does someone emerge from the middle pack to join them?

Blood Sweat and Mini Beers is thick in the middle this year. One could say the league standings have a beer gut. 8 of the 12 teams in the league are sitting in the 700's for points scored. Ian, John T and Ty sit at the top of the middle tier, with each scoring 790+.

Then there is Sko. The collard greens goblin himself has scored 669 fantasy points this year, yet he has lucked his way into 629 points against. He somehow sits at 6-6, even with median scoring. Davante Adams is now on the IR, and Scary Terry is out for the year. Does the mirage evaporate?

Craig Is 3-9 And The Unluckiest Man In The League
His five head-to-head losses came against scores of 141, 138, 155, 139 and 169, and he won the week he scored 140.
By Ty

Fantasy football can be cruel. No league member knows that more this year more than Craig.

Staring up the standings from 12th place, he has scored a respectable 750 points for, good enough to be considered a member of the league's middle pack. His opponents simply have not cared, though. His 877 points against is second most in the league, only behind Ty.

After seeing 141, 138, 155, 139 and 169 put up by his opponents through five weeks, he finally breaks though with 140 points and has a 2-0 week with the median score in his favor. The betting favorite after week five, all eyes will be on Craig to see if his luck has finally turned.
