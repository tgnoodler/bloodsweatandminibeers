Blood, Sweat & Mini Beers

House Rules

No exceptions, and yes, kickers count.

IThe Ice

  1. Any starter who finishes the week at zero or below earns his owner an Ice.
  2. Kickers and defenses count.
  3. The owner drinks a Smirnoff Ice and posts a photo or video as proof.
  4. He has one week from that player's kickoff to do it. After that he owes a penalty Ice on top of the first one.
  5. Bench players can't Ice you.

IIThe jacket and the jumpsuit

  1. The champion gets the jacket and picks next year's trip.
  2. Last place caddies at the golf outing in a personalized jumpsuit.
  3. Last place is decided by the regular-season standings. The losers bracket doesn't save anybody.

IIIThe lineup

  1. You start 1 QB, 2 RBs, 2 WRs, 1 TE, 2 flex (RB, WR or TE), 1 kicker and 1 defense.
  2. You get 5 bench spots and no IR spot.
  3. A player locks when his game kicks off.

IVThe season

  1. The regular season is Weeks 1 through 14.
  2. You play two games a week: one against your opponent and one against the league median. Beat the median score and it counts as a win.
  3. The top 6 make the playoffs, which start in Week 15. The top two seeds get a bye in the first round.
  4. Ties in the standings go to total points, then head to head.

VThe auction

  1. It's an auction. Everybody gets $200 to buy 15 players.
  2. You have 30 seconds to nominate a player, and the clock resets to 10 seconds on every bid.

VIThe wire

  1. Everybody gets $100 in waiver money for the whole season. It doesn't reload.
  2. Claims are blind bids. The high bid wins, and you can bid $0.
  3. Waivers run early Wednesday morning.
  4. A dropped player sits on waivers for 1 day before he's a free agent.
  5. Free agents cost nothing and can be added any time before their game kicks off.

VIITrades

  1. The trade deadline is Week 11.
  2. Trades sit for 1 day before they go through.
